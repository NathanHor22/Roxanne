Subject: Custom ESP32-S3 meeting recorder - design and assembled prototype quotation

Please quote engineering design and 2-unit / 5-unit assembled prototypes for a
battery-powered meeting recorder, including its microphone assembly and harnesses.

Requirements:
- ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8, microSD, approximately 1.3-inch SPI display.
- Four microphones, one speaker; test target 3-5 metres in meeting rooms.
- USB-C charging and programming, protected single-cell rechargeable battery.
- Volume +/-, Record/Stop, BOOT, RESET, and physical power button.
- Orderly SD shutdown followed by hardware removal of system power, also on USB.
- Audio openings around the case, side-facing speaker, side-access microSD/USB.

Please quote two approaches separately:
A0: Custom main board + purchased XVF3800 microphone/processing module.
A1: Custom main board + custom XVF3800 audio board with four plug-in PDM mic heads.

6. Evidence of experience with battery charging, RF modules, MEMS microphone

7. A bring-up and acceptance plan, including 1/3/5 metre recordings with multiple
