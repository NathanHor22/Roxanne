Included in the assembly

  • ESP32-S3 and 1.3-inch screen envelope.
  • ReSpeaker XVF3800 with four installed microphones.
  • Side-facing speaker; SD card exits the right edge.
  • USB-C charging and programming at the left edge.
  • Power, Record/Stop, Volume +/−, BOOT and RESET.
  • 91 × 59 × 5 mm battery below the board.

Power budget

About 3 W recording and 10 W combined peak from the battery. These are calculated allocations, not measured performance.

Regulator stability, charging heat, battery ratings and actual connector footprints still require engineering validation. The model uses simplified component envelopes and provisional supports. There is no routed PCB in this package.
